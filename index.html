About Me

Get to know me!

Hi! My name is Louis and I am a games programmer based in Brighton, UK. My focus is in tools development and gameplay programming. Between university, solo projects and game jams I have used Unreal Engine 5 and Unity extensively and I am comfortable with C++ and C#. My time at Falmouth University also gave me a strong grounding in Git and Agile as well as strong teamwork and communication skills.

When I am not programming games, I enjoy playing games! Some of my favourite games include Outer Wilds, Risk of Rain 2 and Slay the Spire. I also love writing and playing music - having contributed synth and guitar-based original soundtracks to several projects during university. Outside the game-dev world, I spend my time caring for my pet shrimp, listening to progressive rock, metal and electronic music and playing Magic: The Gathering with friends.
